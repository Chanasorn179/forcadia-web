# Surface brief: Forcadia site (all public pages, reader, admin)

Mode: experience. Audience: Thai novel readers, world-explorer fans, first-time visitors. Success: visitors get hooked on the world; the story leads, lore follows.
Constraints: Thai first; Forcadia/Fourcadir names; light and dark themes; real crests and portraits only; no heavy motion; must not look like a template, be hard to read in Thai, or feel too bright or sweet.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every page is a card reading. The prologue is card 0, The Empty Throne; the eight Houses are eight arcana laid in a broken ring around it. Refuses the category default of a dark hero with gold serif over a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Wine-velvet reading cloth as ground (#2a0f1f, light theme: pale lilac linen #ece6ee). Cards are printed card stock: full-field House colours (saffron, ultramarine, viridian, slate violet, teal, vermilion, gunmetal, eclipse black) or bone stock for text, with a thin gold-foil inner frame line (#d9b45a) used as line only, never fill. Rounded card corners, numbered indices in Roman numerals, Thai display serif with loops, Noto Sans Thai body.

STORY: The visitor reads the first lines immediately, turns the card to keep reading, then lifts House arcana to explore characters, cities and lore.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, one large upright card (about 470x690 at 1440) tilted slightly, face up, index "0 · THE EMPTY THRONE", the prologue title and opening lines in large Thai serif, primary action "พลิกไพ่ · อ่านต่อ" on the card. Right, eight House cards fanned in an arc, face down, each lifting and revealing its crest on hover or focus.

FORM: Tarot major-arcana deck, candidate 5 of my ordered list. seed: none (unavailable). Raises: each House owns a full colour field; damped spring motion, one turn per action, still under reduced motion; unpublished content shows as face-down cards; one master grid where pages differ by spread.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
